The defence line: where a contracted supplier's inputs actually originate, tier by tier — and the exact point at which the paperwork stops being evidence and starts being assertion.

The gap is the finding.

Everyone in defence procurement can name their tier-one suppliers. Almost nobody can evidence tier three. The distance between those two facts is where export-control exposure, forced-labour exposure and state-ownership exposure all sit — and a supplier questionnaire returned in full tells you nothing about it.

This line reports where the record stops as a finding in its own right, with a severity, rather than as a caveat at the end of a green dashboard.
