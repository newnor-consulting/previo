What shapes it

The subject

A person and a company are different pieces of work. A company assessment traces an ownership structure and then screens the people it reaches, so it is priced against the size of what it uncovers rather than as a unit.

The depth

A standard assessment covers the subject. A deeper one follows the associates and the connected entities that the first pass surfaces. We will tell you which one your question needs, including when it is the cheaper one.

The cadence

A single appointment is quoted on its own. A programme with a regular cadence is committed against volume, which is where the per-assessment figure falls.

Questions

What you probably
want to know.

Why is there no price list?

Because a per-unit price answers the wrong question. What an assessment costs depends on the subject and the depth the decision needs, and quoting a flat figure would mean either overcharging simple work or under-scoping hard work. Ask us and you will get a number.

Are there tiered features?

No. Every report runs the full assessment for its subject type and is released by a person. What changes across engagements is volume, turnaround priority and the commercial wrapper.

How fast do reports come back?

We give you a timeframe when we quote the work. Reports with findings take longer than reports without, because the review is the point.

What if the candidate withdraws consent mid-flow?

We stop, delete what was collected, and do not bill for the report. Consent is revocable at any point before release.

Do you screen outside the UK and EU?

Sanctions and adverse media are jurisdiction-agnostic. Company and statutory register coverage is the United Kingdom and Denmark, and where a subject sits outside both, the report states that rather than returning a quiet clear. We will quote additional jurisdictions on request.

How is this compatible with GDPR?

Processing is conducted under Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest, balanced against the subject's rights and freedoms. A Legitimate Interests Assessment and a DPIA are provided with programme engagements.
