The method in full →

01
The right subject, first

Identity is resolved before a single search runs, so findings land on your subject and not on their namesake. It is the difference between intelligence and noise.

02
Every finding, sourced and dated

Each entry links to the record it came from and is dated by the event, not the article. Open it yourself. Take it to a regulator, a board, or a tribunal.

03
Scored, and taken apart

A score from 0 to 100 with every deduction itemised, so you can trace the number to the finding and the finding to the source. No black box.

04
Signed by a person

An adjudication pass strips anything that will not stand up. Then a person reads the report and signs it. A person releases every report.
