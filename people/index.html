The decision this serves

Fit and proper is a judgement,
not a checkbox.

You are not usually asking whether a candidate has a criminal record. You are asking whether there is anything in the public record that a regulator, a journalist or a board would find later, and whether you looked before you appointed.

Previo gives you the evidence to make that judgement, dated and sourced, and an audit trail showing you made it. The judgement stays yours: we do not recommend for or against a candidate.

Where it stops

The gaps, named
as specifically as the findings.

Registry coverage is the United Kingdom and Denmark. A subject whose working life sits elsewhere is screened against sanctions and published reporting, but not against a home-country company register, and the report says so rather than returning a quiet clear.

Property coverage is Denmark only. A subject with no Danish connection has no property pass at all.

We do not contact any employer, university or referee. Employment and education are compared, never verified.

Most Danish criminal records are not public and we do not hold them. We report published proceedings and official notices, which is a narrower thing, and we do not present it as a criminal record check.

Where a profile cannot be retrieved or a document cannot be read, that is recorded as reduced coverage. It is never recorded as nothing found.

How the output reads

A score you can take apart.

The report is the product. The number is the shorthand.

A subject begins at 100 and every deduction is itemised, so you can trace the number back to the finding and the finding back to its source. Thresholds move with the seniority of the role — a public-office appointment clears at a higher score than an individual contributor — so the bands below are the default, not the scale.

80–100
Clear

No material findings against the sources reached. Released after review, like every report.

50–79
Caution

Findings that warrant a conversation with the candidate before a decision. Context usually matters.

0–49
Do not proceed

Reserved for sanctions matches, serious regulatory action, or a pattern incompatible with the role.

Consent and lawful basis

The candidate is told,
and can stop it.

The candidate receives a secure invitation and gives consent before anything runs. Consent is revocable at any point before release: we stop, delete what was collected, and do not bill for the report.

Processing is conducted under GDPR Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest, balanced against the candidate's rights, with a Legitimate Interests Assessment available on request. Candidates may make a subject access request, and that is a legal right rather than a courtesy. Retention matches your regulatory obligations.
