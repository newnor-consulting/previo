Pricing

What an engagement costs.

Priced per assessment, by subject and by depth. No feature tiers: every report runs the full source set for its subject and is signed before release. What changes is volume and the commercial wrapper.

What shapes it

The subject

A person and a company are different pieces of work. A company assessment traces an ownership structure and then screens everyone it reaches, so it is priced against what it uncovers rather than as a unit.

The depth

A standard assessment covers the subject. A deeper one follows the associates and connected entities the first pass surfaces. That is the layer where nominee arrangements actually live.

The cadence

A single appointment is quoted on its own. A programme with a regular cadence is committed against volume, and that is where the per-assessment figure falls sharply.

Questions

What you probably
want to know.

Why is there no price list?

Because a per-unit price answers the wrong question. What an assessment costs depends on the subject and the depth the decision needs. Ask us and you will get a number, usually the same day.

Are there tiered features?

No. Every report runs the full source set for its subject type and is signed by a person. What changes across engagements is volume, priority and the commercial wrapper.

How fast do reports come back?

We commit to a timeframe when we quote the work. Reports with findings take longer than reports without, because the review is the point.

What if the candidate withdraws consent mid-flow?

We stop, delete what was collected, and do not bill for the report.

Do you screen outside the UK and EU?

Sanctions and adverse media are jurisdiction-agnostic and run globally. Company and statutory register coverage is the United Kingdom and Denmark, and we quote additional jurisdictions on request.

How is this compatible with GDPR?

Processing runs under Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest, balanced against the subject's rights. A Legitimate Interests Assessment and a DPIA come with programme engagements.
