About

Previo is a product of NewNor Consulting ApS, registered in Copenhagen. We assess people, companies and supply chains against the public record, and we say where the record runs out.

Why the gap is the product.

Most of this industry sells confidence. A clean report reads as a clean subject, and the distance between those two things — everything the check did not look at — is where the real risk sits, quietly, until it does not.

We hold no proprietary database. That sounds like a weakness and is the opposite: it means every finding we give you is one you can open and check for yourself, and it means we have no incentive to imply coverage we do not have. The alternative business model requires you to trust a black box.

So we report two things with equal weight: what the record establishes, and where it stops. The second one is the harder sell and the more useful answer.

How we work

Invite-only, for now

We are onboarding the first engagements deliberately and slowly. If your question is one we cannot answer well, we would rather say so than take the work.

Signed, not automated

A person reads and releases every report. It is a control on the automation, not an exemption from any obligation, and we do not present it as one.

No recommendation

We set out what the record shows. We do not advise for or against a subject, and an assessment that shaded into advice would no longer be an assessment.

Danish law

Our terms are governed by Danish law and the courts of Copenhagen. Personal data is handled under GDPR with the Danish Data Protection Agency as supervisory authority.
