The deliverable

An assessment that marks its own edges.

Most screening tells you what it found. The harder and more useful question is what it looked for and could not reach — because an absence of findings from a source that was never queried is not a clean result, and reading it as one is how due diligence fails.

Every Previo report therefore ends with the same section, and it is not a disclaimer.

Sources reached

  • Named individually, with the date each was queried
  • Each finding linked to the record it came from

Sources not reached

  • Named just as specifically, with the reason
  • Jurisdictions and registers outside our coverage, stated

Method

Six commitments.

The method in full →

01
Resolve the subject before searching

A finding against the wrong entity is worse than no finding at all.

02
Every finding carries its source and its date

You can open the record yourself and check our reading of it.

03
Nothing is inferred

Signals that identify rather than incriminate are recorded and never scored.

04
Coverage is stated

Each report names the sources reached and the sources not reached.

05
A person releases every report

A control on the automation. We do not describe it as an exemption from anything.

06
Where the record stops is itself a finding

Reported with a severity, not buried in a footnote.

Two Danish cases where the evidence
