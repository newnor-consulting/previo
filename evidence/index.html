Evidence

What a report
actually contains.

An assessment is only useful if a reader can take it apart. This is the structure, section by section, and what it commits us to.

Structure

Front matter

The subject as we resolved it, the engagement it relates to, the date the assessment was made, and who signed it. An assessment is a statement as at a date, and it says which date.

The finding

The band and the score, and immediately beneath, every deduction itemised. You can trace the number to the finding and the finding to the record.

Findings by source

Each entry carries what was found, which source it came from, the date of the underlying event, and a link you can open. Weak matches to the subject are marked as weak.

Recorded, not scored

Identity resolution, profile capture and document comparison appear in their own section, explicitly marked as informational. They tell you how confident the identification is. They never move the number.

Coverage and limitations

The sources reached and the sources not reached, each named, with the reason. This section is the reason the rest can be trusted, and it is never omitted for being inconvenient.

Reviewer's signature

The person who read the report before it was released, and any notes they attached. No assessment leaves without one.

From the public record

Two cases where
nobody looked.

Both concern Danish political appointments. In each, material that later ended a career was sitting in company registers, regulator records and national journalism before the decision was taken, and no systematic check was run.

These are readings of the public record, not Previo assessments. We did not screen either individual, and no Previo score has been produced for either of them.

Case 01 · 2026
4 days
Election to expulsion
Four findings, all before the ballot

A bankruptcy with DKK 1.6m in claims, a labour enforcement order, union safety complaints, and municipal permit and billing complaints.
Case 02 · 2022–2023
14 years
Earliest record to crisis
Five findings, all before nomination

Three senior dismissals across three institutions, an alleged forgery, an undeclared benefit, and union complaints from more than thirty former colleagues.

All material in both readings is drawn from published journalism by Danish national media and from publicly accessible government registers. No private or non-public data was used.
